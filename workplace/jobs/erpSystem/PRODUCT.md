# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are admins. They use the system in a day-to-day operations context to create orders, add stock, and print the daily order summary.

## Product Purpose

This system exists to help admins manage products and agents, keep stock current, create orders quickly, and produce a daily order summary they can trust.

## Positioning

A simple ERP for managing products, agents, inventory, and daily order work in one place.

## Brand Personality

Easy, modern, useful.

## Anti-references

No specific anti-references were provided. Keep the experience aligned with the current Thai ERP/admin direction and avoid drifting into decorative or marketing-style UI that slows down operational work.

## Design Principles

- Make the core operational tasks fast to reach from the first screen.
- Keep product, stock, and order information easy to scan at a glance.
- Treat the daily summary as a primary output, not an afterthought.
- Preserve clarity in Thai labels, numbers, and statuses.
- Favor practical workflows over ornamental UI.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Responsive and readable in Thai. No additional specific accessibility requirement was requested.
